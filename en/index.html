About us

Masajemalaga | Chiromassage and Wellness Centre on the Costa del Sol

Masajemalaga is part of augusto, an osteopathy and wellness centre in Málaga city centre. Besides chiromassage, you can book osteopathy, MAM (the augusto movement method) and other natural techniques there, or ask us to come to your home or hotel anywhere on the Costa del Sol.

Find out more about the centre at osteopatiaoctavioaugusto.es and see all its techniques and prices at osteopatamalaga.es.

We design every experience individually, in a calm, welcoming space with every detail cared for, where the only goal is to switch off, release tension and renew your energy.

Masajemalaga was founded by a chiromassage practitioner qualified at the prestigious Kiros school, a lover of sport, the sea and nature, and today we work with a team of therapists who share the same approach. We combine the best classic, deep and sports chiromassage techniques with the latest trends in hands-on wellbeing.

Everything you need to know about your chiromassage in Málaga

Do I need to bring anything for the chiromassage?

You don't need to bring anything. We provide everything you need so your experience is completely comfortable and relaxing.

How much does a chiromassage cost?

All our sessions last 60 minutes. A chiromassage costs €90 (from €65 with a package) at our augusto centre in Málaga city centre, or from €120 at your home.

Are you located near the hotels in Málaga?

Yes: our augusto centre is in Málaga city centre, next to El Corte Inglés, and we also come to your home or hotel. It's ideal if you're on holiday on the Costa del Sol and want to rebalance your body after so much sightseeing, or if you live nearby.

Do you offer couples chiromassages?

Yes, we have special sessions for couples. It's the perfect experience to share a moment of disconnection and complete wellbeing with someone special.

What does a sports chiromassage or chiromassage involve?

Sports chiromassage works the muscles in depth to release tension, support your muscular wellbeing and rebalance your body after sport or a whole day of walking around sightseeing.

Do I need to book an appointment in advance?

We recommend booking in advance to make sure we have availability at the time that suits you best, although we always try to fit in last-minute clients whenever possible.

How can a relaxing chiromassage help me?

Our relaxing chiromassage is designed to help you switch off, let go of everyday stress and enjoy deeper rest, with gentle, unhurried and pleasant movements.

Why choose our center for your chiromassage in Málaga?

We offer a complete wellness experience in Málaga city, tailoring every session to what you need — whether you want to unwind during your holiday or release everyday muscle tension.

Do you offer a chiromassage service for weddings?

Yes, our Wedding Wellness service brings a chiromassage corner to your wedding for the couple, guests and bridesmaids, with professional therapists and a setup tailored to your celebration.

Do you work with wedding planners, hotels or event companies?

Yes, we partner with wedding planners, hotels and event organisers on the Costa del Sol, offering chiromassage corners for their celebrations. If you'd like to collaborate with us, visit our partner companies page.

Do you do chiromassages for corporate events or VIP parties?

Yes, we bring mobile chiromassage to corporate events, weddings and VIP parties in Marbella, Málaga and across the Costa del Sol. Find out more on our VIP chiromassage events page.

Where do osteopathy, MAM, pilates and the other natural techniques take place?

At our augusto centre in Málaga city centre: Pje. Compositor Lehmberg Ruiz, 4, Edificio Santander, Nº 2, Local 9, 29007 Málaga.

How much do the natural techniques cost?

Sessions last 1 hour. Osteopathy, chiromassage and kinesiology: €90, or from €65 with a package. Japanese facial lifting, wood therapy and Brazilian lipodrainage: €70, or from €60 with a package. MAM hybrid session: €150 (members, €120). Personal training: the first group session is free. For the other techniques, we will tell you the price on WhatsApp.

How do I pay for the first appointment?

You pay at the centre, in cash or by card. Nothing to pay when booking.

How far in advance do I need to book?

Appointments are booked at least 24 hours in advance. If you need one sooner, message us on WhatsApp at +34 621 01 59 23 and we'll see if there's a slot.
