A new experience to enjoy your wedding

A wedding doesn't have to be limited to the ceremony, the cocktail hour, the food and the music.

You can add something different so your guests also have a moment to relax, switch off and enjoy themselves.

At Masajemalaga.es we create chiromassage corners and wellness experiences for weddings, for the guests as well as for the bride, her bridesmaids, family and friends.

The service can be included during the preparations, the cocktail hour, the celebration or the party, and it can also be part of a wellness experience lasting the whole weekend.

What is a wedding chiromassage corner?

A wedding chiromassage corner is a space within the celebration where guests can enjoy a short chiromassage while still being part of the event.

You don't need to turn your wedding into a spa or dedicate a large area to the service.

We create a pleasant, discreet and professional area that blends into the surroundings and lets guests sit down, relax for a few minutes and return to the celebration.

How many therapists does a wedding need?

It mainly depends on the number of guests, the length of the service and when you want to offer the chiromassage.

For a small wedding, one therapist may be enough.

For celebrations with more guests we can add two, three or more therapists working at the same time, increasing capacity and reducing waiting times.

You tell us how many guests you have and how long you want to offer the service. We recommend the most suitable set-up.

You don't need a full spa.
