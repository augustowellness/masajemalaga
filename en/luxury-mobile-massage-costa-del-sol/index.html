Everything you need to know about your chiromassage in Málaga

Do I need to bring anything for the chiromassage?

You don't need to bring anything. We provide everything you need so your experience is completely comfortable and relaxing.

How much does the mobile chiromassage cost?

Our luxury mobile chiromassage service starts at €450, including travel within our coverage area. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll send you the full menu of services and prices.

How far do you travel for a mobile chiromassage?

We cover Marbella, Málaga, Torremolinos, Benalmádena, Mijas Costa and Fuengirola, coming straight to your villa, yacht or apartment.

What do I need to have ready at home to receive the chiromassage?

Just a quiet space of about 2x3 metres; we bring the table, linens, oils and ambient music.

Can I book several chiromassages at once for different people?

Yes, we coordinate several therapists in parallel if there are a few of you at the same villa or apartment.

Do I need to book an appointment in advance?

We recommend booking in advance to make sure we have availability at the time that suits you best, although we always try to fit in last-minute clients whenever possible.

Do you charge extra for travelling to me?

The price already includes travel within our usual coverage area; for more distant locations, ask us on WhatsApp.

Why choose a mobile chiromassage instead of going to a centre?

Because you save the travel time there and back, and enjoy the same professional quality in the comfort and privacy of your own accommodation.

Do you come to hotels and holiday rentals?

Yes, we come to hotels, villas, yachts and holiday apartments in Marbella, Málaga, Torremolinos, Benalmádena, Mijas Costa and Fuengirola, with all the equipment needed.
