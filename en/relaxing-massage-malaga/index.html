Everything you need to know about your chiromassage in Málaga

Do I need to bring anything for the chiromassage?

You don't need to bring anything. We provide everything you need so your experience is completely comfortable and relaxing.

How much does a chiromassage cost?

All our sessions last 60 minutes. A chiromassage costs €90 (from €65 with a package) at our augusto centre in Málaga city centre, or from €120 at your home.

How is a relaxing chiromassage different from other types of chiromassage?

It uses gentle, unhurried movements without working deep tissue, focused on calming the nervous system rather than treating specific muscular issues.

Is it suitable if I've never had a chiromassage before?

Yes, it's the option we usually recommend to first-timers, precisely because of how gentle it is.

Does it include aromatherapy or relaxing music?

Yes, it's accompanied by aromatic oils and ambient music to complete the sense of calm.

Do I need to book an appointment in advance?

We recommend booking in advance to make sure we have availability at the time that suits you best, although we always try to fit in last-minute clients whenever possible.

How long does a relaxing chiromassage session last?

All sessions last 60 minutes, just the right time to focus on yourself.

Why choose your relaxing chiromassage in Málaga?

Because we take care of every detail of the atmosphere so the experience is one of real disconnection, not just technique.
